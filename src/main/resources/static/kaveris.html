Heart of Ranchi

Ranchi’s Favourite Pure Veg Destination

From very humble beginnings the group which established the Punjab Sweet House, at the dawn of the Indian Independence, founded Kaveri Restaurant at G.E.L. Church Shopping Complex on Main Road, Ranchi.

Kaveri in no time became the most popular restaurant in the city providing affordable quality, royal vegetarian delicacies, lightning-fast service, and warm family dining. You will find everything from refreshing shakes, chaats, and crispy dosas to sizzling paneer curries and royal thalis.
