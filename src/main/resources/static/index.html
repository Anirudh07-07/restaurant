Our Rich Heritage

Serving Ranchi For Over Seven Decades

From very humble beginnings when the group established Punjab Sweet House in Ranchi at the dawn of Indian Independence, Kaveri has grown into an iconic household name across Jharkhand.

In 1993, Kaveri Restaurant opened its doors at the G.E.L. Church Shopping Complex on Main Road, quickly becoming Ranchi’s most beloved dining destination for pure vegetarian food lovers. Today, our catering division brings the same royal standard to mega weddings and corporate banquets across Eastern India.
