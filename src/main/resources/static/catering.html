50 to 10,000 Guests

Hugely Popular Caterer For Weddings & Grand Galas

Kaveri offers a perfect solution to your events, functions, and wedding receptions. Our caterers are equipped to plan, suggest, and execute flawless culinary arrangements tailored to your guest profile and cultural traditions.

Kaveri is adept at catering from 50 to 10,000 people, at various offsite and onsite locations across Eastern India. With over 25+ years of wedding catering experience, our specialties include 'A-Grade' pure vegetarian multi-cuisine setups, live interactive counters, and heritage Punjab Sweet House dessert buffets.
