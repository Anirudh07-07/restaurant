Punjab Sweet House

Home / Our Group / Punjab Sweet House
Over 75 Years of Tradition

Ranchi’s Legendary Sweet Destination Coinciding with Independence

One of the earliest Indian Sweet Shops in the Eastern region of India saw its birth coincide with India’s Independence in 1947. Since its inception and well over the last seven decades, it has catered to over four generations with the highest quality of traditional Indian sweets and savoury delights.

The iconic restaurant and confectionary outlet has undergone extensive renovation and refurbishment to sport a modern contemporary look while fiercely guarding the age-old recipes, pure cow desi ghee sourcing, and artisanal craftsmanship that made it a household name in Jharkhand.
